01 • THE DESIGN BRIEF & CONCEPT

Crafting Timeless Spatial Harmony

A landmark hospitality destination in the heart of Bangalore, the Araku Coffee Flagship integrates bio-climatic organic design principles with sophisticated commercial detailing.

The ceiling features fluid curved bamboo arches that guide guests through the cupping room, brewing bar, and retail gallery.

Key Execution Highlights:

  • Custom engineered bent-bamboo structural ceiling vaults.
  • Handmade terracotta and terrazzo floor finishes with brass inlays.
  • Specialty brewing counter with integrated drainage and power modules.
  • Acoustic baffling concealed in curved wall treatments to manage crowd noise.
  • Custom retail display millwork with integrated spot illumination.
  • Fluid indoor seating booths with bespoke curved leather banquettes.
