01 • THE DESIGN BRIEF & CONCEPT

Crafting Timeless Spatial Harmony

Created for Arpita & Nithish at V2 Poorvi Enclave, this interior design embraces light-filled airy proportions with sophisticated detailing. An open living and dining area serves as the social anchor, defined by pristine statuario marble flooring and bespoke furniture pieces.

The culinary zone pairs acrylic cabinetry with high-grade quartz countertops, while bedroom suites feature customized wardrobes with integrated sensor lighting and warm textural fabrics.

Key Execution Highlights:

  • Grand open-plan living & dining pavilion with Italian marble flooring.
  • High-performance modular kitchen with seamless quartz island and breakfast bar.
  • Master bedroom retreat with tailored upholstery and backlit wardrobe suites.
  • Integrated smart automation for architectural lighting and temperature control.
  • Bespoke TV unit joinery with concealed cable management and floating credenza.
  • Acoustic paneling and false ceiling cove details in all living zones.
