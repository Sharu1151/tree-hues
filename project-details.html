01 • THE DESIGN BRIEF & CONCEPT

Crafting Timeless Spatial Harmony

Designed for contemporary living, this project at VDB Willow Farm seamlessly blends open-concept spatial planning with bespoke natural wood craftsmanship. The brief required expansive entertaining zones that flow organically into tranquil private suites.

Every element—from the fluted boiserie room partitions to the custom coffered false ceilings with integrated indirect cove illumination—was fabricated in our dedicated joinery studio to millimeter tolerances.

Key Execution Highlights:

  • Monolithic marble dining table with custom brass geometric base.
  • Curved teak wood fluted partition offering visual segregation while preserving daylight.
  • Multi-tier architectural cove illumination with intelligent Lutron automation.
  • Seamless full-height glass sliders connecting the interior lounge to private garden decks.
