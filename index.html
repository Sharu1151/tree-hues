ABOUT US

About Tree Hues

Tree Hues is a dream and hardwork of our founder Sachin Shetty. He is from a village in Mangalore, who began his career in civil engineering and harbored a grand ambition of building his own company. Armed with B.Tech. in Civil Engineering from NITK Surathkal, he slowly developed his interest in construction and interiors when he started his Job in Bangalore 13 years ago.

Tree Hues is built on the idea of creating spaces that feel rooted yet progressive. We believe, design is a balance of timeless character and modern expression.

Our design approach is flexible, allowing us to work across styles while staying true to each client’s needs.

Our process-driven execution and focus on fine details set us apart. With a clear workflow and careful coordination, we ensure every project is delivered efficiently and with quality.

What We Do?

What We Do?

At Tree Hues, we specialize in blending Indian heritage with modern design, but our creativity is not limited to any single style. What sets us apart is our well-organized approach and keen attention to detail during execution. We use a detailed, step-by-step process to ensure that every aspect of your project is handled smoothly and with precision, meeting your expectations.

We place a strong emphasis on post-handover service. We don’t just complete the project and move on; we are committed to supporting you even after the work is done. Our goal is to ensure that everything continues to perform well and meets your needs long after the project’s completion.

values

Principles

Our Core Values

Customer Obsession:

We are dedicated to transforming spaces with exceptional interior design and construction services. Our focus is on you, the customer. We listen to your needs and work hard to exceed your expectations. Your satisfaction is our top priority, and we are obsessed with delivering the best results for every project. We believe in creating beautiful, functional spaces that reflect your style and vision.

Quality:

We are dedicated to delivering high-grade quality in every project we handle. We define quality as paying attention to every detail and using the best materials and techniques. Our skilled team works hard to ensure that every job is done right, from start to finish. We aim to create spaces that are not only visually stunning but also built to last.

Innovation:

We blend innovation with timeless elegance. We believe in combining the best of new ideas with classic design principles. Our team embraces the latest trends and technologies while also honoring traditional craftsmanship and styles. We aim to create spaces that are both modern and enduring, using fresh, creative solutions alongside proven methods. By valuing the old and celebrating the new, we strive to deliver designs that are unique, stylish, and stand the test of time. Our goal is to provide you with exceptional results that honor classic beauty and embrace innovative thinking.

Frugality:

We believe in being smart with our resources. We focus on getting the most value from every rupee spent on interior design and construction. Our team works carefully to avoid waste and use materials and time efficiently. We are dedicated to providing high-quality results while keeping costs under control.

Integrity:

We are committed to being honest, transparent, and ethical in all our interior design and construction projects. We promise to deliver on our commitments and treat every customer with fairness and respect. Our goal is to build trust and provide reliable services that you can count on.
