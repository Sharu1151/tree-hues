01 • THE DESIGN BRIEF & CONCEPT

Crafting Timeless Spatial Harmony

Crafted for Amruth & Kalpitha, the Mirabilis residence balances tactile serenity with functional modern lifestyle demands. Clean rectilinear geometry is paired with soft ambient illumination, creating a peaceful oasis from the bustling city outside.

Carefully curated materials—including natural wood veneers, satin matte finishes, and subtle architectural bronze reveals—unify the living, dining, and private bedroom wings.

Key Execution Highlights:

  • Warm minimalist living lounge with acoustic wall fluting and indirect LED glow.
  • Master bedroom with floor-to-ceiling wardrobe joinery and floating study console.
  • Architectural entrance foyer with bespoke concealed footwear cabinetry.
  • Seamless transition between formal entertainment and private resting quarters.
  • Understated dining nook with designer acoustic pendant illumination.
  • Textured neutral micro-cement accent surfaces throughout.
